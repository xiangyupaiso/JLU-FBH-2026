FROM THE STUDIO / 11 INITIAL DRAFTS

The first drawings.
A story taking shape.

These eleven drafts explore the patient’s question, the molecular explanation and the closing gestures of care. Alongside alternate compositions, handwritten notes reserve space for text. They show the painting and explanation being planned as parts of the same page.

01 / THE PATIENT’S QUESTION
Finding the opening’s focus

Both drafts place the medical student beside the patient’s question, “What if it comes back…”. The foreground patient is softly drawn in one version and more defined in the other. Comparing them shows how a change in emphasis alters the same scene.

02 / TREATMENT & INQUIRY
Drawing the problem, then the question

Treatment concerns gather around the patient’s silhouette in one draft. A comparison sketch sets methods beside their limitations, while a thinking clinician introduces the search for another approach. Seen together, these drawings explore how the patient’s experience can lead into the research question.

03 / PAGE COMPOSITION
Leaving room for the explanation

One map study shows the painting alone. The other adds a pencilled box marked “文字区” (“text area”), reserving a place for the explanation. The annotation shows how the artwork and reading space were considered within the same composition.

04 / MOLECULAR STORY
A familiar ring within the platform

The circRNA study places a ring beside handwritten sketches of stability and expression. The platform drawing returns to that shape, with molecular motifs arranged around it. The recurring circle gives the explanation a familiar starting point.

Conceptual sketches of circRNA stability, expression and the platform, using hand-drawn curves and molecular symbols.

05 / CARE & CLOSURE
Closing with a gesture of care

The patient and medical student meet beneath a sunlit window. In the second drawing, cupped hands hold a figure carrying a heart. Both bring the story back to care through a simple gesture.
