Project description / 2026

One ring. Many targets.

CirCure explores a multi-epitope circular RNA vaccine strategy for TNBC. The design question is whether antigen breadth can be increased while construct identity, expression, and presentation remain experimentally testable.

Design record DR–01
Observation TNBC is heterogeneous.

02 / The biological problem

TNBC is not one thing.

Triple-negative breast cancer contains substantial molecular and immune diversity. A single-antigen strategy can become a fragile promise when different cells do not present the same target—or stop presenting it.

Input layer01

Rank candidates before falling in love with them.

Candidate epitopes should be compared through relevance, predicted presentation, population coverage, safety flags, and redundancy. Computation produces a shortlist; it does not prove immunogenicity.

GateScoring record → shortlist → candidate-level assay plan
Sequence layer02

Give every module a reason to exist.

Epitope order, linkers, translation entry, cassette length, and replacement rules should be visible in one versioned construct map. Modularity matters only if one weak candidate can change without hiding the consequences elsewhere.

GateVersioned sequence logic → alternative designs → build record
RNA layer03

Close the RNA—and prove that it closed.

Circularization is an experimental result, not a visual motif. Product identity, junction evidence, exonuclease resistance, purity, and linear controls are needed before stability or translation can be interpreted.

GateIdentity + RNase R + junction + purity controls
Delivery layer04

Let delivery constrain the design early.

Payload size, dose, expression location, innate immune activation, and manufacturing feasibility determine what the construct can responsibly promise. Delivery is part of the molecular question—not packaging added later.

GatePayload boundary → formulation comparison → uptake readout
Response layer05

Connect expression to presentation, then response.

Protein expression alone does not establish vaccine function. The project must trace the route from encoded product to processed peptide, MHC presentation, and an interpretable candidate-specific immune signal.

GateExpression → presentation → response, with safety beside activity

01 / Why TNBC

A disease label that contains many biological states.

TNBC lacks expression of the three receptors used to define several other breast cancer treatment strategies, yet that shared clinical definition does not make the disease biologically uniform. CirCure therefore begins with diversity as a design constraint rather than a problem to mention after choosing a target.[1]

Project response

Use explicit inclusion, exclusion, and replacement rules for a ranked epitope portfolio.

02 / Why circRNA

A closed molecule creates a testable expression hypothesis.

Circular RNA has been investigated as a stable antigen-expression platform in preclinical systems. For CirCure, that literature motivates a comparison—not a shortcut. Molecular stability, translation, immune activation, delivery, and therapeutic benefit remain different claims with different evidence requirements.[3,4]

Comparison required

Identity, purity, translation magnitude, expression duration, and innate immune response.

03 / Construct logic

The final project documentation must show the actual translation element, epitope order, linker logic, circularization strategy, sequence versions, and controls. 04 / Boundaries

The unknowns belong inside the project story.

Delivery feasibility, long-term immune safety, manufacturing, population coverage, affordability, and clinical workflow remain open. The Human Practices page records how clinical, technical, ethical, and access feedback turned several of these unknowns into design constraints and explicit no-go claims.
