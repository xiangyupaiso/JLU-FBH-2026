Abstract

CirCure requires computational evidence at three connected decisions: which epitopes deserve experimental attention, whether a circular RNA construct is structurally plausible, and which expression conditions should be tested first.

This page is an information-architecture prototype, not a scientific results claim. It demonstrates how the final wiki can document data, assumptions, equations, parameter provenance, validation, uncertainty, and the exact design decision produced by each model. Placeholder panels remain intentionally empty until the team provides citable inputs.

System overview

Model architecture

Three models answer three different design questions. Their outputs become experiment-ready decisions, not isolated figures.

Central question

How can computation reduce the number of uncertain CirCure designs entering Wet Lab while preserving a transparent record of why each candidate was kept, revised, or rejected?

Evidence maturity.

Every claim advances only when the corresponding evidence exists. This prototype is currently at the proposed stage.

2.3 Proposed scoring formulation

If the team uses a multi-criteria score, publish the normalized terms and weight rationale. A schematic formulation could take the following form; it must be replaced if the actual method is different.

Eq. 1
Si = wIIi + wCCi + wPPi − wAAi − wTTi

Here, I, C, and P represent proposed benefit terms; A and T represent proposed risk terms. Define every term, range, unit, normalization, and weight source in the final manuscript.

Technical appendix 2A · Assumptions and weight rationale Show details
  1. Scores must be comparable only after an explicit normalization rule.
  2. Missing evidence must not silently become a neutral score.
  3. Weight selection requires either literature support, expert elicitation, or sensitivity analysis.
  4. Hard safety exclusions should occur before ranking when scientifically justified.

4.1 Minimal system

Begin with the smallest model that can answer the experimental question. A schematic three-state system is shown only to reserve the final equation layout. The team must replace species and kinetics with its actual biological mechanism.

Eq. 2

dm/dt = ksyn − kdegm

Eq. 3

dp/dt = ktlm − kpp

Eq. 4

dr/dt = f(p, θ) − krr

m, p, and r are placeholders for construct abundance, translated product, and a measurable response. Do not publish this system unless it matches the final mechanism and data.

07

Interpretation boundary

Limitations

This prototype contains no team-supplied Dry Lab dataset, fitted parameter, validated equation, model output, or experimental result. It therefore cannot support claims about efficacy, stability, immunogenicity, safety, or clinical performance.

The equations and model names reserve a publication structure only. The final team should delete any module that does not correspond to real work, then replace every proposed element with citable, reproducible evidence.
